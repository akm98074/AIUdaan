Where you are now

The level where there is no map

The Staff or Principal promotion that keeps slipping. The move into your first director or VP seat. The manager-to-IC pivot nobody teaches you to run. The plateau where you know you're capable of more but can't see what's holding you back. And underneath all of it, the quiet worry that you're not adapting to AI fast enough.

In 2023, as a Director, I went back to an IC role after 22 years away from one. It became the most instructive stretch of my career. AI wasn't ready yet, so I used it to survive the transition and built the intuition that became my edge. That is what I teach.

This is not advice from a distance. It works like a gym coach — for the technical and the non-technical both. Here's how the work runs:

  • We diagnose the real gap. Technical, but also your environment and the people around you.
  • We pick a real project and work it together over three to six months.
  • I give you blunt feedback, and we map the whole board, not just the code.

What makes it different

I've been doing this for ten years, through the pre-AI era and now the AI one. What makes it different from most coaching is simple: I don't just advise — I work on your goal with you. For every hour we spend one-on-one, I spend an equal hour between sessions doing the work behind it: researching your situation, finding the right material, and customizing it to where you're trying to go. Some of that is technical. A lot of it isn't — the org dynamics, the stakeholder map, the way you're read by the people who decide promotions. That's usually where the real gap is.

Over the last decade I've worked with more than 100 engineers, managers, directors, and partners at the largest tech companies. Many earned a hard promotion. Others got unstuck, changed direction, or grew into VP and partner roles.

They don't just get promoted — they come out AI-native, the kind of person AI makes more valuable, not less. I take on a small number of clients at a time.
