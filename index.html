The pitch

How it works

This is not advice from the sidelines. We take your goal and run it like a project.

Say you can't get a skeptical org to move on your ideas. Or you're clearly doing the work, but the Staff case never quite lands. Either way, we treat it as the project: scope it at a high level, map the stakeholders, and build a real plan. Who to talk to and in what order, the doc that makes your thinking legible, the strategic frame that turns an opinion into a case. Then we work it week by week, I give you blunt feedback, and we adjust as reality pushes back.

Everything we build is yours, and we work from your real situation without needing anything you can't share. No confidential design doc, nothing against policy. The stakeholder map, the influence plan, the strategy doc, the talking points for the conversation you're dreading: we make those together, for you.

The other half of the work

Because it runs like a project, you leave each session with specific tasks to move it forward, the draft to write, the conversation to have, the thing to try before we next meet. Between sessions I do the other half. I research your exact situation and send resources chosen for it, not a generic list. Sometimes that is strategic or organizational, just as often technical, because real depth and breadth are how senior people earn the room and make the case for the next level. For every hour we spend one on one, I spend an equal hour on the work behind it. I've been doing this for ten years, through the pre-AI era and now the AI one.
