FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?
  • Staff, Principal, and Distinguished engineers
  • Managers, from first-line up through Director and VP
  • People converting between IC and management, in either direction
  • Anyone I've mentored before. You already know how I work
Who is this not for?
  • Anyone who wants interview cramming, a resume rewrite, or answers from a distance
  • Anyone who wants reassurance more than the truth. I give blunt feedback, and this only pays off if you can take it and act on it
  • Anyone looking for a light-touch monthly check-in. This is hands-on and asks real work of you between sessions
Can you get me promoted in three or six months?

No. Senior promos run on a 12 to 18 month cycle you don't fully control: calibration windows, committee, your skip's capital, budget. Anyone promising a title inside a quarter is selling you a story.

What you do control is the case, the stakeholder map, the visible project, and the skill that closes the gap. That is what actually moves a promo, and it's the part most people get wrong or skip. In three to six months you won't be handed a title. You'll be on the path, visibly running the case, with skills and momentum that outlast our last session. The promo lands when the cycle turns, and by then you have the case and the skills to close it, with or without me.
