The pitch

How it works

This is not advice from the sidelines. We take your goal and run it like a project.

  1. 1

    We scope the project

    Map the stakeholders and build a real plan: who to talk to and in what order, the doc that makes your thinking legible, the strategic frame that turns an opinion into a case.

  2. 2

    We work it week by week

    You leave each session with specific tasks — the draft to write, the conversation to have, the thing to try. I give you blunt feedback and we adjust as reality pushes back.

  3. 3

    I do the other half

    Between sessions I research your exact situation and send resources chosen for it, not a generic list. For every hour we spend one on one, I spend an equal hour on the work behind it.

Everything we build is yours, and we work from your real situation without needing anything you can't share — no confidential docs, nothing against policy.
